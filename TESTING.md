# Bantay Basura — Beta Testing Guide

Thanks for helping test! 🙌 This is an early build. Everything you do here is
**test data** on a separate sandbox — feel free to poke around and break things.

## Where
**https://bantay-basura-beta.vercel.app**

- Works best on your **phone** (open in the browser; you can "Add to Home Screen").
- **No account needed.** Reporting is anonymous.

## What to try
1. **Browse the map** — pan around, zoom, tap the layers/filter controls.
2. **Search a place** — top-left "Search places" (try your town or barangay).
3. **Locate me** — the crosshair button (allow location when asked).
4. **Report waste** — tap **Report Waste**, drop the pin, add a photo + category
   + details, and submit. Should take under a minute.
5. **Verify a report** — open a report and try "Still here" / "Looks clean".
6. **Explore the pages** — Transparency, Impact, How It Works, Resources, About.

## Please don't
- Upload anything sensitive or inappropriate — photos are **not moderated yet**.
- Put real personal info in reports (it's a public map).

## Sending feedback
- In-app: tap the **feedback icon** (bottom-right, just under the zoom buttons).
  Pick Bug / Idea / Confusing, write a line, send. It's anonymous.
- Or just message me directly.

Tell me: what confused you, what broke, what felt slow, and what you'd want next.

## If it looks unchanged after I push an update
Tap the **"New version available → Refresh"** toast when it appears, or fully
close the tab and reopen. (The app caches itself to work offline.)
